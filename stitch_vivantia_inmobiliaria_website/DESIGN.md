## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-end, architectural digital presence tailored for luxury real estate transactions on mobile web platforms. Drawing inspiration from deep emerald tones, architectural line work, and warm satin gold accents, the brand personality projects prestige, solidity, clarity, and absolute discretion.

Targeted at discerning property buyers, high-net-worth sellers, and premium renters, the interface avoids the visual clutter common in mass-market property aggregators. Instead, it creates an atmosphere reminiscent of an editorial architectural monograph combined with a bespoke private concierge service.

The design movement is **Editorial Minimalist Luxury**:
- **Structured Precision**: Generous negative space paired with razor-thin hairline borders in muted champagne gold and deep forest green.
- **Architectural Rhythm**: Proportions inspired by contemporary facade design, emphasizing clean vertical lines, framed photography, and deliberate typographic scale.
- **Warm Understated Tactility**: Warm off-white surfaces prevent sterile corporate coldness, establishing an inviting, tactile environment where properties feel aspirational yet tangible.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the visual identity derived from architectural emerald and satin gold materials.

- **Primary (`#0F382C`)**: Deep Forest Emerald. Serves as the primary anchor for dominant elements, key branding headers, high-priority buttons, and critical active states.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`)**: Satin Champagne Gold. Used with surgical precision for status badges, property highlights, active tab indicators, and tactile accents.
- **Tertiary (`#134E3A`)**: Deep Sea Pine. Provides layered depth in dark card surfaces, navigation headers, and hover/pressed states.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1A2421`)**: Charcoal Pine. Provides high-contrast readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Background & Canvas**: Primary app surface is warm alabaster (`#FAF8F5`), supported by pure optic white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards and soft mist (`#F4F5F4`) for section alternations.
- **Border Gradients & Lines**: Muted metallic gold border tones (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.24)`) and subtle pine lines (`rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.08)`) frame listing modules and forms without competing with imagery.

## Typography

Typographic treatment relies on a deliberate juxtaposition between architectural high-contrast serif headlines and clean, geometric sans-serif UI copy.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical prestige and luxury editorial flair. Used for main hero propositions, section titles, property prices, and prominent testimonial statements.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides crisp legibility on small-screen touch surfaces. Its open apertures and modern metrics maintain clarity even in dense feature lists (sqm, room counts, amenities) and legal disclosures.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-Labels**: Set in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.08em`), utilizing the secondary satin gold or neutral dark at reduced opacity to establish crisp categorical hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first fluid vertical scroll, transitioning to a structured 4-column layout on mobile web and 12 columns on desktop viewports.

- **Vertical Cadence**: Core layout rhythm follows an 8pt base grid. Spacing is intentionally generous between thematic blocks (`space-2xl` / 56px) to maintain an unhurried, luxury browsing experience.
- **Screen Margins**: Mobile viewports utilize an outer canvas margin of `1rem` (16px), allowing maximum photographic surface area while safeguarding tap regions. Tablet and desktop stretch to `2rem` (32px).
- **Horizontal Carousels**: Property cards within mobile sections use bleeding carousels with negative margins and a trailing snap offset of `space-md` (16px), indicating further inventory without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach combining subtle tonal layering with whispered warm shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base)**: `#FAF8F5` base background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a hairline border `1px solid rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.06)` and an ambient, warm downward drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Modals, Quick Search Bar, Bottom Bars)**: `#FFFFFF` or translucent `#0F382C` with backdrop-filter blur (`16px`). Shadow increases to `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.12)`.
- **Luxury Gold Accent Layer**: Key featured tags and VIP markers use a subtle rim lighting effect via `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.45)`.
- **Floating Consignment Trigger**: Anchored quick-action trigger incorporates a satin directional diffusion: `0 8px 24px rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (Level 1)** architectural corner treatment:

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, listing tags) use `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) border radii.
- Property cards and primary container surfaces feature `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- Modals, action sheets, and sheet drawers utilize `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) strictly on top corner boundaries.
- Pills and full radii are reserved exclusively for compact status tags (e.g., "En Venta", "Exclusiva", "Verificada") to provide distinct organic contrast against rectilinear architectural photography.

## Components

### 1. Hero Search Engine (Comprar / Vender / Arrendar)
- **Segmented Control**: Segmented bar seated inside an off-white enclosure with a dark pine active tab indicator (`#0F382C`) and gold underline.
- **Search Card**: Compact floating module containing:
  - Property Type select (Apartamentos, Casas, Penthouse, Lotes).
  - Geographic zone dropdown (e.g., El Poblado, Llanogrande, Chapinero Alto).
  - Price range selector with instant currency formatting.
  - Primary CTA: High-contrast button in `#0F382C` with gold label icon.

### 2. Property Cards (Destacados & Portafolio)
- **Ratio & Treatment**: Aspect ratio 4:3 or 16:9 for cover photos with a subtle top-right badge overlay ("Exclusiva Vivantia" in champagne gold on translucent pine glass).
- **Meta Row**: Clean iconographic row displaying bedrooms, bathrooms, and square meters with clear dividers and labels.
- **Pricing**: `Playfair Display` font in neutral dark with secondary exchange rate or administration fee in `body-sm`.

### 3. Value Proposition Matrix ("Sin Letra Chica")
- Three-column icon and narrative cards emphasizing transparent commission, zero hidden administrative fees, and full legal certification.
- Background in subtle `#F4F5F4` with 1px border `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`.

### 4. Fast Consignment Module ("Consigna tu Inmueble")
- Multi-step simplified mobile form:
  - Property type chip selection.
  - Neighborhood autocomplete input.
  - Expected valuation slider.
  - Direct WhatsApp / Concierge submit button styled in dark forest emerald with gold trim.

### 5. Buttons & Controls
- **Primary Action**: Deep emerald `#0F382C`, white text (`#FFFFFF`), subtle hover transition to `#134E3A`.
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent surface with `1px solid #C5A059` border and `#0F382C` typography.
- **Inputs**: Warm white base `#FFFFFF`, hairline border `#E2E4E2`, active focus state with `#C5A059` halo ring and zero layout jitter.

### 6. Testimonials & Geographic Coverage
- **Quote Cards**: Minimalist layout featuring client quotes in italicized `Playfair Display`, accompanied by verified transaction tags (e.g., "Vendido en 24 días").
- **Regional Coverage Map/List**: Pill-shaped geographic filters showcasing primary metropolitan hubs with direct listing counters.
